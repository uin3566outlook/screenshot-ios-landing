CLIPSHARE / LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Last updated · September 28, 2026 / Application provider · Huanwei Ye

Overview

ClipShare lets you capture, crop, annotate, save, and share images on your iPhone. This policy covers the iOS app and its public legal pages. Huanwei Ye provides the app. For privacy questions, email airproductlab@gmail.com.

Images and on-device processing

Images you choose with the system photo picker or pass through the Quick Capture Shortcut are processed on your device. Cropping, drawing, text, arrows, masking, and export do not upload your image to a developer server. The app keeps working files temporarily to complete editing or sharing and removes them when no longer needed. On launch, it removes its own orphaned temporary image files older than 15 minutes. A copy you explicitly save to Photos or send to another app remains under your control at that destination.

Photos permission

Choosing an image uses Apple's system photo picker without requiring access to your entire photo library. Only when you tap Save to Photos does the app request add-only Photos permission and write the edited image to your library. You can deny or change that permission in iOS Settings; sharing remains available.

Information saved on your device

The app stores appearance preferences, Quick Capture setup status, and favorites you create in local app storage. Favorites can contain crop coordinates, annotation effects, names, and text you typed, but do not contain the original image. The developer does not receive this content. You can clear all favorites in Settings; removing the app deletes its local container under iOS behavior. Deleting a favorite or the app does not remove copies previously saved to Photos or shared elsewhere.

Sharing and third parties

When you tap Share, iOS presents available destinations. The edited image goes only to the destination you choose. That destination and its provider handle the image under their own terms and privacy policy. ClipShare does not keep a list of recipients. The app uses no account system, ads, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking.

Public legal pages

The app can load the current Privacy Policy and Terms of Use from GitHub Pages over HTTPS. The developer adds no cookies, forms, analytics scripts, or advertising to these pages. A normal web request may disclose your IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, and time to GitHub and its infrastructure so the page can be delivered and protected. No screenshot, favorite, or app content is attached to that request. GitHub describes its processing in the GitHub Privacy Statement. If the site is unavailable, the app shows its bundled copy.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request, troubleshoot, and meet applicable obligations. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. Your email service and ours process messages under their own policies. Please do not send sensitive screenshots unless needed. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to legally required retention.

Your choices and changes

You can manage favorites in the app, Photos access in iOS Settings, and shared copies at the destination you selected. Because we do not hold your app content on a developer server, we cannot retrieve or delete it for you. This general-audience utility is not designed to collect children's personal information. We will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure if data practices change. The date above identifies this version.
