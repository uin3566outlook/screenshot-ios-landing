CLIPSHARE / LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Last updated · October 12, 2026 / Application provider · Huanwei Ye

Overview

ClipShare lets you capture, crop, annotate, save, and share images on your iPhone. This policy covers the iOS app and its public legal pages. Huanwei Ye provides the app. For privacy questions, email airproductlab@gmail.com.

Images and on-device processing

Images you choose with the system photo picker or pass through the Capture and Share shortcut are processed on your device. Cropping, drawing, text, arrows, masking, and export do not upload your image to a developer server. The app keeps working files temporarily to complete editing or sharing and removes them when no longer needed. On launch, it removes its own orphaned temporary image files older than 15 minutes. A copy you explicitly save to Photos or send to another app remains under your control at that destination.

Camera permission

The app requests camera access only when you choose Take Photo. Capture opens the editor directly. The original photo is held in memory for that editing session and is not automatically saved to Photos or History. Closing the editor releases it. Only an edited result you explicitly save to Photos or successfully share can be retained as described below. The camera feature does not request microphone or location access.

Photos permission

Choosing an image uses Apple's system photo picker without requiring access to your entire photo library. Only when you tap Save to Photos does the app request add-only Photos permission and write the edited image to your library. You can deny or change that permission in iOS Settings; sharing remains available.

Pasting images

The home screen uses Apple's system paste button to receive an image when you tap it. When you tap Paste & Edit in the desktop widget, the app reads the clipboard after entering the foreground and opens the copied image directly in the editor. iOS may ask you to allow pasting from another app. Ordinary app launches and returns do not automatically import clipboard contents. The widget itself does not access the clipboard. The pasted original is used in memory for the current editing session and is not automatically saved to Photos or History. Only an edited result you explicitly save or successfully share can be retained under the storage rules above. Pasting does not request Photos or camera access.

Manual redaction and saved annotations

You can cover areas manually with mosaic, blur or solid color. Image editing takes place on your device. Automatic redaction is currently unavailable and does not scan images or run a PII model. Previously saved masks and review decisions remain editable annotations; favorites may retain their category, coordinates and review state, but not recognized text. History stores flattened edited images under the storage rules below.

Information saved on your device

The app stores appearance preferences, Quick Capture setup status, and favorites you create in local app storage. Favorites can contain crop coordinates, annotation effects, names, and text you typed, but do not contain the original image. After a share completes or you save to Photos, the app also keeps the edited image in private on-device History. History has a 100 MB capacity with no image count limit and removes older images when capacity is exceeded; you can delete individual records in the app. The developer does not receive this content. You can clear favorites in Settings; removing the app deletes its local container under iOS behavior. Deleting a history record or the app does not remove copies previously saved to Photos or shared elsewhere.

Sharing and third parties

When you tap Share, iOS presents available destinations. The edited image goes only to the destination you choose. That destination and its provider handle the image under their own terms and privacy policy. ClipShare does not keep a list of recipients. The app uses no account system, ads, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking.

Public legal pages

The app can load the current Privacy Policy and Terms of Use from GitHub Pages over HTTPS. The developer adds no cookies, forms, analytics scripts, or advertising to these pages. A normal web request may disclose your IP address, browser or device information, requested URL, and time to GitHub and its infrastructure so the page can be delivered and protected. No screenshot, favorite, or app content is attached to that request. GitHub describes its processing in the GitHub Privacy Statement. If the site is unavailable, the app shows its bundled copy. If you choose to open component-source or license links, those websites may process ordinary visit metadata. These requests do not include images, recognized text or other app content.

Support email

If you email support, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request, troubleshoot, and meet applicable obligations. We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. Your email service and ours process messages under their own policies. Please do not send sensitive screenshots unless needed. You may request deletion of support correspondence, subject to legally required retention.

Your choices and changes

You can manage favorites in the app, Photos access in iOS Settings, and shared copies at the destination you selected. Because we do not hold your app content on a developer server, we cannot retrieve or delete it for you. This general-audience utility is not designed to collect children's personal information. We will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure if data practices change. The date above identifies this version.

Purchases and entitlement verification

The app uses Apple StoreKit to verify purchase transactions on your device and read Pro access. It shares an access display snapshot with widgets on the same device. It does not create a developer account, use purchases for cross-app tracking, or upload images or edits because of a purchase. Apple handles account, payment, and transaction records under its privacy policy. The app does not receive your Apple Account password or full payment-card details.
