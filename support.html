Quick Capture setup

Open Settings in ClipShare and choose Quick Capture to review the one-time Shortcuts setup and trigger options. You can also choose an image from Photos or try the sample without setting up a shortcut.

Photos and local data

Save to Photos asks for add-only permission only when you tap that action. You can change it in iOS Settings. Favorites are stored on this device and can be removed with Settings > Clear All Favorites. Images you shared or saved elsewhere must be managed at their destination.

Contact

For app, privacy, or terms questions, email airproductlab@gmail.com. Include the app version, iOS version, and a brief description of the issue. Please do not send sensitive screenshots unless needed.
